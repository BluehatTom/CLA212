Design Thinking Learning Lab

Six interactive simulations · Independent cases · Accessible learning support

Practice empathy, problem framing, ideation, selection, prototyping, and iterative testing. Every lab includes two fictional cases: Jordan’s campus-events experience and Elena’s Riverbend customer-pickup experience. Complete either case or both. No progress transfers are required.

Each lab has an optional Review concepts and vocabulary panel. Learning artifacts download as tagged PDFs with an HTML alternative.

1 · Interview Simulator

Collect meaningful evidence through realistic branching questions and feedback.

Open Interview Simulator

2 · Problem Framing

Separate evidence, assumptions and counterevidence; frame a human-centered challenge.

Open Problem Framing

3 · Idea Generation

Develop varied ideas and respond creatively to design constraints.

Open Idea Generation

4 · Idea Selection

Evaluate alternatives and explicitly discuss tradeoffs and assumptions.

Open Idea Selection

5 · Rapid Prototyping

Follow three clear steps to design and preview an editable service journey.

Open Rapid Prototyping

6 · Test & Iterate

Plan a test, interpret a simulated observation, edit your prototype and retest.

Open Test & Iterate
