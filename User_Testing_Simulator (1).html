User Testing Simulator

Practice asking questions that reveal what users actually experience, need, and do.

How this lab works

You will interview several simulated stakeholders. In each scenario, choose the question you think will produce the most useful evidence.

The simulator will show how the user might respond, identify the type of question you asked, and explain why some questions reveal more than others.

What makes a strong user-testing question?

Strong questions usually ask about specific experiences, behaviors, decisions, barriers, and past actions. They avoid leading the user toward a preferred answer.
